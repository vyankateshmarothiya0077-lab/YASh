Our Story — Nagpur

Born from the Soil of Vidarbha

❋ ❋ ❋

In 1999, with a single government contract and an unwavering belief in the promise of Nagpur's future, Shri Gurukrupa Builders was founded in Dharampeth, Nagpur. The name itself — "Guru's grace" — reflects our founding principle: that every structure we build carries the blessings of craftsmanship, integrity, and community.

From that single project, we grew to become one of Nagpur's most trusted builders and government contractors — empanelled with Maharashtra PWD, NMC, MIDC, and NHAI. Our work spans the Orange City's urban corridors to the remotest villages of Umred and the Vidarbha hinterland.

Today, with 180+ completed projects, ₹120 crore+ in project value, and 5,000+ families housed, we stand as the pioneer of next-generation construction in Nagpur and across Maharashtra.
